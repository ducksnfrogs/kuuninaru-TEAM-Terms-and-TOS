KuuGPT — Privacy Policy Effective Date: October 25, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains what information KuuGPT (the "Bot"), owned and operated by くうになる TEAM, collects and how it is used. 1. Information We Collect When you interact with the Bot (for example, by using a slash command), we may receive and store: - Your Discord user ID - The Discord server (guild) and channel ID where the command was used - The content of the command/interaction itself (e.g., search terms you provide) We do not collect your Discord username or display name beyond what Discord automatically provides with an interaction, and we do not read or store messages outside of direct Bot interactions. 2. How We Use Information Collected information is used only to: - Operate the Bot's features (e.g., searching and returning game data) - Prevent duplicate announcements via internal tracking - Diagnose and fix technical issues We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. 3. Data Storage & Retention Data is stored on servers we operate and is retained only as long as necessary for the Bot to function. You may request deletion of data associated with your Discord user ID at any time (see Contact below). 4. Third-Party Services The Bot operates on Discord's platform and is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy (https://discord.com/privacy). The Bot also retrieves publicly available game data from Project Sekai's content delivery servers; this does not involve sharing your personal data with that service. 5. Children's Privacy The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from users under Discord's minimum age requirement. 6. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in our support server where practical. 7.
